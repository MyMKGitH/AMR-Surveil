# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, assume good faith, provide constructive feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and distinguish scientific disagreement from personal criticism. Respect privacy, data licences and research integrity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, deliberate misrepresentation of scientific evidence, or sharing private data without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code. Report concerns privately through the maintainer contact channel listed on the GitHub profile.
